First Aid Instructions

CPR Instructions

Check the scene for safety and the person for responsiveness

Call 911 or ask someone to call if the person is unresponsive

Begin chest compressions - push hard and fast in the center of the chest

Give rescue breaths if trained (30 compressions to 2 breaths)

Continue until help arrives or the person starts breathing

Burn Treatment

Cool the burn with cool (not cold) running water for 10-15 minutes

Remove jewelry or tight clothing before swelling occurs

Cover loosely with sterile, nonstick bandage or clean cloth

Take over-the-counter pain relievers if needed

Do not apply butter, oil, or ice to the burn

Severe Bleeding

Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth

Elevate the injured area above heart level if possible

Add more cloth if blood soaks through - don't remove the first layer

Call 911 if bleeding is severe or doesn't stop after 10 minutes

Keep the person warm and calm until help arrives
